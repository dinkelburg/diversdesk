## 00:00 Welcome and introduction to the new planner

This tutorial explains how to use the New Planner in daily operations. The example day is 13 August with 15 participants: 13 fun divers and two course participants. The tutorial covers the drag-and-drop workflow first, then the bulk edit bar at the bottom of the planner.

## 00:30 Drag and drop functionality overview

Use the header-row checkbox to select all participants. To select a range, click the first row, hold Shift, then click the last row. Hold Control to add an individual row to the selection. After selecting one row, press a numeric key such as 4 to select that many rows including the first selection. This is useful when the guide-to-participant or instructor-to-participant ratio is four to one.

Drag selected fun divers to the Schedule Trip box. This opens the Schedule Trip sidebar. Selecting a 3 Dive Trip template immediately schedules everyone and updates availability. The trip column shows trip initials and start time; hover to see its full name. When no template exists, choose New Trip, create the trip directly, and optionally save it as a template. Use the ellipsis menu in the planner to manage existing templates.

## 01:00 Shortkeys and selection methods

The selection shortcuts support fast operational planning: select all from the header row, select a continuous group with Shift, add any individual row with Control, or press a number key after selecting a row to select a group of that size. The numeric selection workflow is especially practical when assigning groups according to the configured guide ratio.

## 03:47 Trip template management and editing details

Selecting a trip opens its details. The Overview tab is intended for the fields that most often change: departure time and dive sites. Click either field to edit it. Sites can be added or deleted from this overview. Use the ellipsis menu to edit all other trip fields; boat trips can include more fields than shore dives.

Trip Focus shows the participants on a selected trip. Show All returns to all participants scheduled that day in a deduplicated view. Course participants can be scheduled separately, for example with a Pool Morning template for Open Water day one. Its availability can be configured, such as one instructor plus four participants.

## 07:41 Scheduling inbox and staff focus view

The left panel also contains Activities, Staff, and the Scheduling Inbox. Activities lists the day's activities and can be collapsed. Staff shows assignments. The Scheduling Inbox counts participants who are not yet on a trip; the target is to get this inbox to zero.

To demonstrate the inbox, select everyone and drag them to Drop to Unschedule, then choose the trips to remove. Schedule the fun divers back to a boat trip and the course participants to Pool Morning. Once every participant is scheduled, the inbox reaches zero. If participants still need a guide or instructor, use the provided link to jump directly to Staff Focus, or change view with the control at the top.

Staff Focus has its own Assignment Inbox. Assign guides and instructors by selecting participants and dragging them to the appropriate staff member. Participant lists can be sorted by certification level. When a configured maximum ratio is exceeded, such as assigning a fifth fun diver to a guide with a four-to-one maximum, Diversdesk shows a toast warning and a red indicator on that staff member. In Show All, hovering the red indicator shows the scheduled versus maximum ratio. Reassign a participant to resolve the warning. When staff assignments are complete, the Assignment Inbox reaches zero.

## 11:47 Bulk edit bar functionality and multi-trip unscheduling

For multi-trip planning, first unassign staff with Drop to Unassign and then unschedule participants with Drop to Unschedule. Select the relevant trips in the dialogs when participants belong to more than one trip. Trips can be deleted from their Overview ellipsis menu, or by selecting a trip and pressing Delete; press D to confirm a delete dialog or C to cancel it.

The bulk edit bar supports scheduling many selected participants at once. Select fun divers, choose Select Trip, then New Trip to create a single-dive morning trip. A guide can be selected directly in the bar, but do not assign one guide to a group that would exceed the guide ratio. Course participants can be scheduled to Pool Morning and assigned an instructor in the same bulk-edit action.

## 15:51 Multi-trip scheduling and staff allocation

Create or select the remaining noon and afternoon trips. Filter to fun divers, use the header row to select all filtered participants, and select all three trips in the bulk edit bar. Applying the change schedules the group to every selected trip. In Show All, participants remain deduplicated: each person appears once, with trip badges for the three assignments.

For staff allocation across multiple trips, switch to Staff Focus and select a group in the deduplicated view. Assigning a guide through the bulk edit bar opens a confirmation dialog because the selected participant may be on multiple trips. Confirm all trips, or exclude a trip when the staff member is unavailable. Use the Assignment Inbox to complete the remaining guide and instructor assignments. Drag-and-drop follows the same multi-trip confirmation flow.

## 20:05 Week and month view demonstrations

Week and Month views show staff members and their trip allocations. Click a staff badge to de-duplicate the view into each individual trip, including its departure time and participant count. The Day view's Trip tab provides a trip-by-trip breakdown of participants and assigned guides. Expand or collapse individual trips, and hover participant, trip, or staff badges for further details.

## 24:00 Activities with multiple trips and night dive example

Participants can join multiple activities on the same day. In the example, Miguel joins a three-dive day trip and a night dive with Brett and Jimmy. Opening the activity card can jump directly to the New Planner with that activity expanded.

Select the night-dive participants and use the bulk edit bar to create a new trip when no suitable template exists. The example creates Night Dive - North Shore as a shore dive, single dive, with a 7 PM departure, maximum capacity of five, and saves it as a template. Assign a guide in the same workflow. Return to the Participants tab for a deduplicated view of all participants and guide allocations.

## 25:30 Advanced features: collapse, staff color settings, and AI search

Use the left-panel collapse button to reduce scrolling. Hover collapsed trips to see their details and continue dragging participants to them. This works in both Trip Focus and Staff Focus. In the expanded Staff Focus panel, use the ellipsis menu to open staff color settings. Assign colors individually or use auto-assign for all staff.

The date picker is collapsed by default. Select a date to navigate and close it automatically; selecting the current date also closes it. Filters show active filter badges that can be closed easily. New Booking, New Trip, and Rest Day remain available from the usual controls. The documentation hub contains more guidance, and its AI search can be opened with Control+K to search or ask questions.
