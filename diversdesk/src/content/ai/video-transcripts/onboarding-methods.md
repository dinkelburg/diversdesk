## 00:00 Overview of the three onboarding methods

Diversdesk supports three paperless onboarding methods for customer registration and waivers. Method one is booking-driven onboarding: create a booking and share it with the customer. Method two is generic customer registration, also called walk-in registration, where a participant registers without a booking. Method three is direct booking or instant booking, where customers register through a website or a booking URL shared from Diversdesk.

Booking-driven onboarding is the most commonly used method. Generic registration is optimized for walk-ins and busy moments when staff do not have time to create a booking immediately. Direct booking lets customers register and book themselves online.

## 01:00 Booking-driven onboarding and creating a booking

Create a booking from the Planning page with Create Booking or directly from the Bookings page. CRM users commonly start from Bookings because they may not have access to Planning. Select the activity and enter a booking reference, participant count, pickup details, meeting time, start date, discount, customer message, internal note, and booking source.

Use the activity filters to find the right activity. A descriptive booking reference, such as the main booker's name and guest count, makes bookings easier to find later. Pickup address can be left empty to take it from customer registration, or entered using a hotel or accommodation name. Customer messages can use templates; the top template is added by default and can be cleared. Internal notes are visible only to staff.

## 03:43 Booking source, partner pricing, and add-ons

Booking source is stored on the Metrics page and helps identify where bookings originate, such as Instagram or a partner. For a partner or OTA with different pricing, hide prices from customers before sharing the booking.

Add-ons are configured on the Add-on page and can be attached to the activity. An add-on can be displayed to participants directly on the registration form to upsell items, such as a dive computer.

## 05:31 Payments and payment links

Add payments from the Payments section of a booking. For payment methods that are not integrated through a payment gateway, select the method, save it, and manually mark it as paid after payment is received. The booking status then updates, for example to Deposit when a balance remains.

For a remaining balance, add a payment URL using a payment link generated by the provider. The customer view displays it as a payment button. Integrated payment gateways automate payment status updates and email notifications. Existing payments can be edited to change the date or deleted.

## 08:09 Sharing bookings and scheduling registered guests

Share a booking using the QR code, copy-and-share option, or a message copied to the clipboard. Configure the copied message in General Settings. After sharing, return to Planning on the activity start date. Participants may remain unscheduled until they register, or staff can schedule them immediately according to their preferred workflow.

Diversdesk sends an email when a participant registers; its link opens the relevant booking. In Planning, assign registered participants to a guide and a trip. Trip templates make recurring trips available from a dropdown. Selecting a trip updates capacity, and the trip details show crew, sites, and scheduled participants.

## 10:48 Walk-in registration with the generic registration page

For walk-in onboarding, open Onboarding > Forms and select the Generic Customer Registration Page. Onboarding journeys must already be configured. In the Form Builder, enable the checkbox that makes a journey appear on the generic registration page.

Print the generic QR code so customers can register at any time and choose the onboarding journey that describes them. The selected journey presents the correct registration fields and waivers. The flow is mobile optimized. When a guest registers, they appear as a pending participant in Planning, with an indicator on the participant icon.

## 12:41 Creating a booking from a walk-in registration

Open the pending participant to see the onboarding journey, registration state, and waiver completion. From the registration page, connect the customer to an activity. Enter the booking reference and participants, select the start date, adjust discount or booking source, and submit the booking. The pickup address can already be populated from the guest's registration.

Add a payment if needed, such as a fully paid cash payment, and manually mark it paid when the method is not integrated. The booking returns to Planning as an unscheduled participant. Assign the participant to an existing trip and guide; participants in the same activity are grouped on one activity card.

## 15:31 Direct bookings through My Page or a website

For direct bookings, open My Page and share the My Page URL, an individual activity link, or the Book Now link. Customers select an activity, start date, and participant count, complete the registration form and required paperwork, then save the booking. They can share the booking with fellow participants so those guests can register too.

Direct bookings are shown in a darker blue color on the Bookings page and generate an email notification. Staff can open the booking to add payments or additional items.

## 17:57 Using a shorter registration form for direct bookings

By default, a direct booking uses the regular registration form associated with the activity. To reduce drop-off, create a dedicated onboarding journey for direct bookings in Form Builder by copying an existing journey and keeping only essential fields, such as first name, last name, date of birth, and phone number.

Connect this shorter direct-booking onboarding journey through General Settings. Refer to the documentation hub or contact Diversdesk for help setting up direct bookings.
