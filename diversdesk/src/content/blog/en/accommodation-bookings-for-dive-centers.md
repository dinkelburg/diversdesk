<h2 id="2. Use dynamic price rules and high-season pricing for dive resort bookings" class="section-heading">2. Use dynamic price rules and high-season pricing for dive resort bookings</h2>

Good accommodation management isn't just about setting one price and leaving it there. Demand changes throughout the year, so your pricing should too. You might have different rates for high and low season, charge a little more during weekends or holidays, offer single occupancy supplements, or create special dive and stay packages.

In my opinon, dynamic price rules also help dive centers sell the right room at the right price and avoid manual pricing mistakes that reduce revenue. With Diversdesk, all of these pricing rules can be managed in one place. You can easily set different rates for high and low seasons, adjust prices for weekends or holidays, apply single occupancy supplements, and customize package pricing to suit your business. Everything is flexible and can be updated whenever your pricing changes.
