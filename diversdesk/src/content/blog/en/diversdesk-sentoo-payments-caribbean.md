Between organising boat trips, teaching courses, and preparing rental equipment, your team already has plenty to coordinate. Keeping payments connected to bookings helps reception answer a simple question: what still needs to happen before this guest is ready to dive?

Our Sentoo integration gives dive centres in supported locations a way to connect that booking process with a payment provider built around Caribbean banking.

<h2 id="where-sentoo-is-supported" class="section-heading">Where is Sentoo supported in the Caribbean?</h2>

Sentoo currently lists the following six locations in its [official payment coverage](https://sentoo.io/business/payment-methods/sentoo):

- **Aruba**
- **Bonaire**
- **Curaçao**
- **Saba**
- **St. Eustatius**, also known as Statia
- **Sint Maarten**, listed by Sentoo as St. Maarten

Availability depends on participating banks and your merchant setup. This coverage refers to these Dutch Caribbean locations; availability elsewhere in the Caribbean needs to be checked separately.

Sentoo lists participating banks including Aruba Bank, Banco di Caribe, Caribbean Mercantile Bank (CMB), MCB Bonaire, MCB Curaçao, Orco Bank, and Windward Islands Bank (WIB). The bank choices shown to a customer depend on the merchant's connected bank accounts, as explained in [Sentoo's customer guidance](https://sentoo.io/).

<h2 id="pay-through-a-local-bank" class="section-heading">Let local customers pay through their bank</h2>

Sentoo's Pay by Bank option lets customers approve a payment through their own online or mobile banking environment. They choose an available bank and authorise the transaction there, with the payment details passed through for them.

For a resident booking a course or a returning local diver arranging another trip, this offers a way to pay using a participating local bank account. Customers need an eligible account with online banking access. [Sentoo's business FAQ](https://sentoo.io/support/faq/business) explains the requirements for customers and merchants.

For your dive centre, the value is in connecting a locally relevant payment option with the booking you are already managing in Diversdesk.

<h2 id="payments-in-your-booking-workflow" class="section-heading">Make payments part of preparing the booking</h2>

Diversdesk brings customer details, booked activities, payment history, and outstanding balances together. Your team can review what has been paid while checking the other arrangements for the visit.

Think of a local customer reserving a course for the following week. With Sentoo enabled for your account, you can offer the available payment flow as part of that booking. Reception can review the payment information alongside the course details when preparing for the guest.

That connection is useful for more than the first payment. If the booking changes or the guest adds another activity, your team still has one place to review the bill and the remaining balance.

<h2 id="local-divers-and-visiting-guests" class="section-heading">Plan for local divers and visiting guests</h2>

Your customers may include residents, visiting friends and family, and divers travelling from overseas. Their payment needs can differ. Pay by Bank is useful for customers with a participating local bank account; visiting divers may need another option.

Sentoo also offers card processing through supported acquiring banks, with an e-merchant account required. Its [card payment guidance](https://sentoo.io/blog/accept-card-payments-with-sentoo) describes those arrangements. Talk to us about which payment methods can be enabled for your Diversdesk account, based on your island, bank, and customer mix.
