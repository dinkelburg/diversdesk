Whether you run reef trips in Cozumel, organise cenote dives around Tulum, or teach courses in Playa del Carmen, collecting payment is part of preparing for your guests. A clear payment process helps your team see which bookings have a deposit and which balances still need attention before the diving begins.

<h2 id="stripe-payments-in-mexico" class="section-heading">A payment gateway for dive centres in Mexico</h2>

Diversdesk integrates with Stripe so eligible businesses in Mexico can accept online card payments through their booking flow. Mexico is included in [Stripe's supported countries](https://stripe.com/global), and our team can help you get the integration enabled for your dive centre.

For guests, this means being able to pay online as part of organising their visit. For your team, it means managing the booking and its payment information in the same system you use for the rest of the day.

<h2 id="collect-payments-before-arrival" class="section-heading">Collect a deposit before the first dive</h2>

An advance booking gives you time to arrange instructors, reserve equipment, and plan boat space. Collecting a deposit gives both you and your guest a clearer commitment before those preparations begin.

Diversdesk supports partial or full payment requirements at checkout, based on your setup. You can use that flexibility to fit the way you sell your activities, whether you need a deposit for a course or payment in full for a scheduled trip.

For example, a guest booking a dive course can pay the required deposit online before travelling. Your team can then review the payment history and outstanding balance in the booking while preparing the guest's schedule and equipment.

<h2 id="keep-bookings-and-payments-together" class="section-heading">Keep bookings and payments together</h2>

Payment information is most useful when the person handling the booking can find it easily. Diversdesk keeps payment history connected to the booking, alongside the activities and customer information your team needs.

That helps with everyday questions at reception:

- Has this guest paid a deposit?
- What balance remains on the booking?
- Which payments have already been recorded?

Your team can review those details from the booking page and follow up with the guest when needed. It is a practical way to make payment handling part of your normal booking routine.

<h2 id="prepare-for-arriving-guests" class="section-heading">Give guests more time to enjoy their arrival</h2>

When guests can complete payment steps before their visit, there is less to work through at reception. Combine online payments with Diversdesk's customer registration and digital waivers, and more of the preparation can happen before arrival.

That leaves your team better placed to welcome guests, check their equipment needs, and talk through the diving ahead. The booking process becomes part of the service you offer from the first enquiry.
