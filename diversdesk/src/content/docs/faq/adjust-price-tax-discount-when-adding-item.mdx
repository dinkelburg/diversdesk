When adding an activity or another item to a booking, select **Show more** in the side panel to access its additional settings. Here you can adjust the **price**, **number of dives**, **tax**, and **discount** before adding the item.

## How to Adjust the Values

1. Open the booking and start adding the activity or product.
1. Select the rate or product you want to add.
1. In the side panel, select **Show more**.
1. Update the price, number of dives, tax, or discount as required.
1. Review the resulting amount and add the item to the booking.

## Why Are These Fields Under Show More?

The fields are grouped under **Show more** to keep the side panel focused on the information used most often. They remain available whenever you need to override or refine the pricing details for the item being added.

:::tip
Always review the resulting amount before confirming the item, especially after changing tax or applying a discount.
:::
