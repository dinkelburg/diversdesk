The **Registration Page** (participant page) is where you review and manage participant-specific registration details.

You can open it directly from **Day Manifest**:

1. Click a participant name to open the side panel.
2. Click the participant name hyperlink in the side panel to open the participant registration details page.

## What You Can Review

Typical information available on the registration page includes:

- participant identity and contact details
- registration field completion
- waiver-related details
- uploaded documents and participant notes

## Pickup Address Behavior Linked to Registration

Registration data can be used for pickup logistics when a booking is configured with a pickup meeting type.

If a booking pickup address is left empty during booking creation:

- the system can auto-fill the booking pickup address from registration data
- the source is the **first registered participant** in that booking

For group bookings, this means the first person who completes registration sets the initial pickup address for the booking. This address can still be changed later from Day Manifest.

## Related Shortcuts from Day Manifest

From the Day Manifest side panel:

- participant name hyperlink -> opens the registration details page
- booking reference hyperlink -> opens the booking page

Use this flow when operations staff need to switch quickly between daily execution and full registration detail checks.
