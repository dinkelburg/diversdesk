## Welcome

You can access the documentation hub at any time through the `Support` menu item within your Diversdesk or Traveltruster environment.

![How to open the Support menu](/images/navigating_to_the_support_page.png)

## Best Practice

We recommend going through steps 3, 4, and 5 of the [Quickstart Guide](/getting-started/quickstart-guide/#3-personalize-your-company-page-and-enter-activities). That gives you the configuration baseline needed for the intended Diversdesk setup experience.

## Search Bar

The search bar at the top of the documentation hub helps you find the right topic quickly.

## Guiding Images

To keep the guides compact, many screenshots are shown in mobile format. The same functionality still applies on desktop.

:::tip[Can't Wait?]
If you want to create your first activity right away, jump straight to [Planner](/user_manual/activities/planner).
:::

## Questions, Unclarities or Feedback

If you have questions, need assistance, or want to share feedback, [reach out](https://www.diversdesk.com/contact). We are usually quick to respond.
