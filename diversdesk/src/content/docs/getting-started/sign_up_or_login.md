Use the `Sign up` or `Login` buttons in the top-right corner of the screen to create or access your account. This guide covers both account creation and login.

![Desktop and mobile login buttons](/images/desktop-or-mobile-login-buttons.avif)

## Create an Account

You can create an account through a secure email verification flow or through an existing Google account.

> Already have an account? Continue reading at [Login to your account](#login-to-your-account).

### 1. One Time Password (OTP)

Signing up can be done through the sign-up button on [app.diversdesk.com](https://app.diversdesk.com/u?toggle_modal=account&persist_flow=signin) or, for Diversdesk white-label users, via the personal URL we provided to you.

The `Send a One Time Password` button sends a time-limited 6-digit password to the email address you entered.

1. Fill your email and press 'send the the OTP password'.
2. Fill the OTP password that's sent to your email.
3. You are now logged in. The icon color indicates your logged in status.

![Create an account through a verification email link](/images/sign-up-steps.avif)

Verifying your email will automatically log you into your account. Once logged in, you can access your company page through the hamburger menu in the top-left corner.

:::note[Note]
The hamburger menu and its associated pages will only appear once your email is linked and you've been granted appropriate user rights.

Please [contact us](mailto:info@traveltruster.com?subject=Issues%20viewing%20my%20company%20page) if you experience difficulty viewing your company pages.
:::

### 2. Google Sign-Up

Alternatively, you can sign up using your Google account.

1. Sign up through your google account.
2. Select the desired Google account.
3. You are now logged into your account.
4. *Pls press the 'Continue Using Google' button a 2nd time if your browser blocks pop-ups.*

![Create an account through google](/images/Create_an_account_through_Google.png)

:::note[Note]
It occasionally happens that pop-ups are blocked in your browser. Pressing the button a second time usually resolves this issue. If the problem persists after the second attempt, please get in touch with us.
:::

## Login to Your Account

To log in, visit [app.diversdesk.com](https://app.diversdesk.com/u?toggle_modal=account&persist_flow=signin) or, for Diversdesk white-label users, use your personal URL and select the login button on desktop or the profile icon on mobile.

From there, follow the prompt to access your account.

1. Login through the profile icon
2. After a successful login, you will find all menu items under the hamburger menu in the left top of your screen.
3. Expand to see all the menu items.
4. The profile icon on the right top is a good indication you are logged in.

![Steps to login into your environment](/images/Login_steps.png)

### 1. Email Verification Link

Experience a secure passwordless login that sends you a unique login URL. You can open that URL on the device you are logging in from or on any device where you can access your email.

1. Send the verification link
2. Follow the url to verify your email
3. You are logged in and this newly opened tab can be closed
4. The icon color indicates you’re logged in

![Create an account through a verification email link](/images/Login_with_verification_email.png)

### 2. Google Login

If you already have a Google account, you can log in without creating a separate password.

1. Sign up through your google account.
2. Select the desired Google account.
3. You are now logged into your account.
4. *Pls press the button a 2nd time if your browser blocks pop-ups.*

![Login through google](/images/Login_through_google.png)

### 3. Password

You can establish a password for the conventional login method, ensuring seamless access across multiple devices. Please ensure the use of a robust password to enhance the security of your account.

![Create or reset your password](/images/reset_password.png)
