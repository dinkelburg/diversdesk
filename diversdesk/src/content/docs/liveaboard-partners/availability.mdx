Open **Partner portal → Availability**. For the full booking process, start with the [partner manual](/liveaboard-partner-portal/).

## Find a departure

Use **Operator**, **Vessel**, **Status**, dates, and **Guests** to narrow the list. Hide trips without enough space where offered. Check the **Updated** time and use **Refresh** if the page has been open for a while.

Open a trip to review dates, itinerary, available cabins, occupancy choices, prices, and promotions. Mandatory fees payable onboard are separate from the booking amount collected upfront.

If an operator or vessel is missing, ask the operator to check your access. Permission to view availability and permission to place options can differ.

## Departure status

**Confirmed** describes the departure, not your booking. **Tentative** means operator approval is required before your booking can proceed, even if your usual agreement allows direct booking.

## Cabins and spaces

Check the cabin type, number of guests, and offered occupancy arrangement. The view distinguishes available spaces, checkout holds, options, and booked spaces. Your own reservations are identifiable; other partners' booking details remain anonymous.

## Checkout holds

Opening a trip or selecting a cabin does not reserve it. **Hold cabins & continue** starts a temporary checkout hold, with the duration shown by the countdown.

Complete the booker details and choose **Review option → Place option** before it expires. Closing the panel does not complete checkout: use **Continue checkout** to resume. When the timer expires, the spaces are released.

To change the selection, release the unfinished hold using its release action, then select again. The released spaces become available to others.

## After placing an option

The portal opens **Reservations**. Follow the booking's approval requirement and option deadline there. No customer payment is collected during this checkout. Read [Reservations](/liveaboard-partner-portal/reservations/) for the next action.
