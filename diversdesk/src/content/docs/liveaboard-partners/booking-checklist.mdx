Follow the [partner booking manual](/liveaboard-partner-portal/) for instructions and links to the detailed articles.

1. **View availability.** Open the departure and check cabins, dates, prices, and onboard fees.
2. **Place option.** Choose **Hold cabins & continue → Review option → Place option**. Check the reservation's approval requirement and option deadline.
3. **Confirm booking.** Once the required customer payment is received and you are ready to commit, use **Confirm booking**. This accepts the operator-payment schedule. Open **Partner settlement → View partner invoice** immediately; no email request is needed.
4. **Send paperwork.** Open **Guest registration and paperwork → Copy booking URL**, then share the link. Copying does not send an email. Partner-billed prices are not displayed; separately charged customer extras may be visible.
5. **Pay the operator.** Transfer the amount due using the invoice instructions, then **Report deposit transfer**. The operator still needs to review reported payment proof.
6. **Pay the balance.** Check the remaining amount and deadline, pay, and **Report balance transfer** where available. Check that payment and guest paperwork are complete.

If the operator collects a payment directly, follow that reservation's collection instructions. Check Reservations even when no email arrives.
