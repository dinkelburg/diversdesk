Use this checklist when arranging a liveaboard trip for your guests at [app.diversdesk.com](https://app.diversdesk.com). For the full explanation and visual flow, open the [partner portal guide](/liveaboard-partner-portal/#your-booking-journey).

## 1. Prepare the booking

- Confirm the departure, itinerary, and travel dates with your guest.
- Check cabin availability and the required occupancy arrangement.
- Have the guest and contact details requested by the booking flow ready.
- Review the quoted price, supplements, fees, and your agreed partner terms.

If the price or cabin arrangement is unclear, resolve it with the operator before proceeding.

## 2. Start the checkout hold

Open the trip, choose cabins and guest counts, and click **Hold cabins & continue**. This starts a temporary checkout hold. Simply opening the trip does not hold any space.

Complete the next step before the countdown ends. If you close the panel, use **Continue checkout** to resume. If the timer expires, the spaces are released and you need to select available cabins again.

## 3. Place the option

Enter the main booker's first name, last name, and email. Indicate whether the booker is travelling. Additional guest details can be completed now or provided later according to the operator's instructions.

Choose **Review option**, check the details, and click **Place option**. No payment is collected in this checkout. The portal opens **Reservations**, where you can see the resulting status and next deadline.

## 4. Follow approval and deposit requirements

- **Approval pending:** wait for the operator's decision and watch the approval deadline. Tentative departures also require approval. A declined or expired approval request releases the spaces.
- **Deposit pending:** follow the payment instructions before the deadline. An unpaid deposit can cause cancellation and release the spaces.
- **No approval or deposit required:** the booking confirms immediately after submission. If just one step is required, complete that step.

Check who collects each payment. If you collect it, use **Record deposit** or **Record balance payment** only after receiving the money and when the action is enabled. If the operator collects it, follow their instructions. Uploaded manual payment proof still needs operator review.

## 5. Check confirmation and prepare for departure

Look for **Confirmed** in Reservations before confirming the booking to your guest. A confirmed booking can still have an outstanding balance. Check its amount and deadline, and complete outstanding guest information.

Check the portal even if no email arrives: notifications depend on the operator's settings.

## 6. Handle questions or changes

Use **Release option** and **Confirm release** if that action is available and you no longer need an unpaid option. Otherwise, contact the operator with the reservation reference and requested change. Confirm the applicable terms before promising a change or refund to a guest.
