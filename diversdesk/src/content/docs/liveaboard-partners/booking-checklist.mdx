Use this checklist when arranging a liveaboard trip for your guests. The actions available in your portal depend on the access and terms configured by the operator.

## 1. Prepare the booking

- Confirm the departure, itinerary, and travel dates with your guest.
- Check cabin availability and the required occupancy arrangement.
- Have the guest and contact details requested by the booking flow ready.
- Review the quoted price, supplements, fees, and your agreed partner terms.

If the price or cabin arrangement is unclear, resolve it with the operator before proceeding.

## 2. Track an option

Where available, use an option to hold inventory temporarily while arranging the booking. Note its expiry and complete the required next step before that deadline.

An option is time-limited. If it expires, recheck availability with the operator before promising the space to your guest. Ask the operator if you need more time.

## 3. Review the reservation

When submitting a reservation, check the departure, cabin choice, guest details, and booking total. Keep the reservation reference and review the status and any follow-up instructions.

Before confirming the trip to your guest, make sure the operator's confirmation requirements have been met. If the status or next step is unclear, contact the operator with the reservation reference.

## 4. Follow payment and guest registration instructions

Check who is responsible for payment under your partner agreement and which deposit or balance deadlines apply. Follow the payment methods and instructions provided for that booking.

Where a guest payment flow allows manual payment proof, the operator reviews the proof before accepting it. Check the resulting payment status rather than treating an upload alone as payment approval.

Follow the operator's instructions for guest registration and any outstanding guest information. Review booking emails for requested actions and deadlines.

## 5. Handle questions or changes

For a change or cancellation, contact the operator with the reservation reference, departure date, and requested change. Confirm the applicable terms and availability with them before promising a change or refund to a guest.
