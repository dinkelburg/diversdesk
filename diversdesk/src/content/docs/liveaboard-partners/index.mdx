Use the partner portal to review liveaboard departures and cabin availability, place booking options, and submit reservations with the operator you work with.

This guide is for **approved sales partners and agents**. Access, available inventory, prices, and booking terms depend on your arrangement with the liveaboard operator.

## Get access

Use the portal link provided by your liveaboard operator. If you have not received a link, cannot sign in, or cannot see the operator's departures, contact the operator to check your partner access.

Keep your portal link and this guide bookmarked so you can return to them when preparing a booking.

## Check a departure

Before offering a trip to your guests, review:

- The itinerary, departure dates, and trip duration.
- The available cabin types and occupancy options.
- The prices and any supplements, fees, or promotions shown for your booking.
- The booking and payment terms agreed with the operator.

The portal shows availability from the operator's reservation inventory. Other bookings remain anonymous. Recheck availability when you are ready to proceed.

## Make an option or reservation

An **option** is a time-limited hold while you arrange the booking. A **reservation** is submitted against the inventory the operator has made available to you. Check its status and the operator's requirements before telling your guest that the booking is confirmed.

Follow the [booking checklist](/liveaboard-partners/booking-checklist/) for the information to prepare, option deadlines, and payment follow-up.

## Need help?

Contact your liveaboard operator about partner access, availability, prices, booking terms, changes, or cancellations. Include the departure date and reservation reference, if you have one. For a portal error, include the page you were using and the error message.
