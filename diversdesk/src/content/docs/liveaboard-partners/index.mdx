Arrange your guests' liveaboard trip in six steps. Your reservation shows the applicable amounts and deadlines; check it before each action.

**Start here:** sign in at [app.diversdesk.com](https://app.diversdesk.com) using your invited email address, then open **Partner portal** in the menu.

## 1. View availability

**Where:** Partner portal → **Availability**.

Choose the operator, vessel, dates, and number of guests. Open a departure to check its itinerary, cabins, prices, and any fees payable onboard.

**What happens:** you can see the available spaces. Opening a trip does not reserve them.

**Next:** choose your cabins and place an option. [Availability page details](/liveaboard-partner-portal/availability/).

## 2. Place option

**Where:** the departure panel in **Availability**.

Choose cabins and guest counts → **Hold cabins & continue** → enter the requested booker details → **Review option → Place option**. Finish before the checkout countdown ends.

**What happens:** the portal opens **Reservations**. No payment is collected during this checkout. If the operator must approve the request, wait for approval before confirming. A tentative departure also requires approval.

**Next:** open your reservation and check its option deadline. Confirm before it expires. [Options and statuses](/liveaboard-partner-portal/reservations/).

## 3. Confirm booking

**Where:** **Reservations** → open your reservation → **Partner settlement → Confirm booking**.

Once you have received the required customer payment and are ready to commit, review the agreed payment schedule and confirm.

**What happens:** confirmation secures the booking, issues your partner invoice, and makes you responsible for paying the operator by the agreed deadlines. This action does not charge the customer or record a separate customer payment receipt. The reservation may show **Settlement pending** while payment to the operator is outstanding.

**Your invoice is available immediately:** open **Partner settlement → View partner invoice** in the same reservation. You do not need to request it or wait for an email.

**Next:** share the guest paperwork link. If **Confirm booking** is unavailable because the operator collects payment, follow the reservation's payment instructions. [Payment arrangements](/liveaboard-partner-portal/payments/).

## 4. Send paperwork

**Where:** open the reservation → **Guest registration and paperwork → Copy booking URL**.

Send the copied link to the main booker or guests using your preferred channel. It opens the whole booking, where guests can complete their registration and required paperwork.

**What guests see:** booking and guest-registration information. Partner-billed items show **Through partner** instead of your partner prices; separately charged customer extras may still show prices.

**What happens:** copying the URL does not send an email. Guests with a listed name select it and verify their email; guests without a listed name use **Register** for an available place.

**Next:** arrange payment to the operator by its deadline. Do not delay payment while waiting for paperwork. [Paperwork and guest details](/liveaboard-partner-portal/paperwork/).

## 5. Pay the operator and report the transfer

**Where:** **View partner invoice** for the amount and payment instructions; **Report deposit transfer** in the reservation or its actions menu.

Transfer the amount due to the operator, then open **Report deposit transfer** and follow the instructions to report it.

**What happens:** a reported transfer awaiting review is not yet a recorded payment receipt. Check for **Deposit transfer awaiting operator review** and follow up if the operator needs more information.

**Next:** check the remaining balance and its deadline. If the booking requires one full payment, follow that payment's instructions. [Invoices and transfers](/liveaboard-partner-portal/payments/).

## 6. Pay the balance

**Where:** open the reservation and check the balance amount and deadline.

Transfer the remaining amount according to the invoice, then choose **Report balance transfer** when available. Follow the collector shown if the operator collects the balance directly from the customer.

**What happens:** the balance stays outstanding until payment is recorded. A transfer awaiting review still needs operator review.

**Complete:** check that no payment remains due and all guests have completed the required paperwork.

## Emails and help

Emails depend on the operator's settings. **Reservations is your place to check progress even if no email arrives.** You can access the invoice and share paperwork without waiting for an email.

For booking changes, payments, access, or deadlines, contact your operator. For portal problems, contact [support@diversdesk.com](mailto:support@diversdesk.com).
