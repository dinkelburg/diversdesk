Open **Partner portal → Reporting** to review the booking and settlement information available to your partner account.

## Review the figures

Review the summary for each currency: **Booking total**, **Paid**, **Commission earned**, **Operator owes you**, and **You owe operator**. The booking table lists the reference, operator, amounts, commission, settlement, and direction. Use pagination to review further bookings.

## Open an invoice

Use **Download invoice** on a booking row where an invoice has been issued. You can also find it through **Reservations → your reservation → Partner settlement → View partner invoice** after confirmation. There is no need to request the invoice by email.

## Act on a booking

Use **Reservations** to check current booking details, deadlines, guest paperwork, and transfer actions. A reporting figure does not replace checking whether a reported transfer is still awaiting operator review.

For a question about a figure or a settlement, contact the operator with the relevant booking reference. For the complete process, return to the [partner manual](/liveaboard-partner-portal/).
