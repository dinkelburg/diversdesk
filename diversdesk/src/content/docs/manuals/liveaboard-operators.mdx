Follow this sequence for liveaboard operations. Use each booking's actual status, payment schedule, and guest details when deciding the next action.

## 1. Prepare departures and cabins

Open **Liveaboard → Trips & availability** and the relevant departure. Review dates, itinerary, cabin inventory, occupancy choices, prices, supplements, and onboard fees before offering spaces for sale.

**Result:** the departure and its sellable spaces are ready for reservations.

## 2. Check partner access and booking rules

Open **Agents/Partners** and review your partner accounts and agreements. Check which vessels each partner can access, whether bookings need approval, who collects the deposit and balance, and the agreed settlement terms. Review the liveaboard journey's enabled emails and recipients.

**Result:** partners receive the access and booking process you intend. Share the [partner manual](/help/partners/) with them.

## 3. Review reservations and requests

Open **Liveaboard → Reservations** and review requests, options, cabin allocations, and deadlines. Approve or decline requests that require your decision before their deadlines. Review new and changed reservations against the departure inventory.

**Result:** approved requests can proceed to the applicable confirmation or payment step.

## 4. Follow confirmation and settlement

Check the booking's confirmation state and payment schedule. A partner can confirm their commitment while payment to you remains outstanding; this is **Settlement pending**. Partner confirmation issues their invoice. Review reported transfer evidence and record payment receipt only when the money is received.

**Result:** the booking and settlement statuses reflect the actual stage reached. A booked reservation can still have a balance due. [Booking page reference](/user_manual/bookings/booking-page/).

## 5. Complete guest registration

Review guest details and paperwork progress. Share the booking link or follow the configured guest communication flow. Check missing details and required forms before departure.

**Result:** guests have the information and paperwork needed for the trip. [Guest registration methods](/workflows/registration-methods/).

## 6. Prepare the departure

Review cabin allocations, guest readiness, outstanding balances, and the trip's operational information. Follow up on outstanding items and confirm the final arrangements with your team.

**Result:** you have a clear view of the departure's bookings and remaining tasks.
