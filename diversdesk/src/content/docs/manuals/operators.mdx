Use this manual to move from setup to daily operations. Open the linked reference articles for detailed page controls and explanations.

## 1. Sign in and set up your business

Sign in at your Diversdesk address or [app.diversdesk.com](https://app.diversdesk.com). Open **Menu → Settings → General** to check company details and booking defaults. Add team members and appropriate permissions through **Settings → Users**.

**Result:** your team can access the environment. [Setup guide](/getting-started/quickstart-guide/).

## 2. Add what you sell

Open **Activities → Pricing** to configure activities, prices, and add-ons. If applicable, configure rooms through **Accommodation → Calendar**, plus rental and retail inventory in their respective sections.

**Result:** your products are ready to use in bookings. [Activity setup](/user_manual/activities/activity-setup/).

## 3. Prepare guest registration

In **Settings**, configure the **Form Builder**, **Waiver Builder**, and **Activities List (Forms)**. Connect the appropriate paperwork to your activities.

**Result:** guests are asked for the information and waivers relevant to their bookings. [Registration workflows](/workflows/registration-methods/).

## 4. Create and review bookings

Use your booking and planning pages to create the booking, add the correct activities or items, and check dates, guest counts, and prices. Open the booking to complete customer details and review its contents.

**Result:** the booking appears in your operational overview. [Bookings page](/user_manual/bookings/).

## 5. Share the booking and follow payments

Open the booking and share its customer link for registration and applicable payment steps. Review registration and payment statuses in **Bookings**. Record payments only when received, using the appropriate payment action.

**Result:** you can see which guests or payments still need attention. [Booking details](/user_manual/bookings/booking-page/).

## 6. Prepare and run the day

Open the **Planner** and **Day Manifest** to review guests, activities, and assignments. Check outstanding paperwork, payments, equipment, and staffing before the activity starts.

**Result:** your team has the information needed for the day's operations. [Planner](/user_manual/activities/planner/) · [Day Manifest](/user_manual/activities/day-manifest/).
