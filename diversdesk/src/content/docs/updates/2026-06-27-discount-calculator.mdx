Release Date: June 27, 2026

## Discount Calculator Is Here

The new **Discount Calculator** replaces the old one-field discount workflow with a more flexible rule-based system.

You can now create reusable discount rules, decide whether they should apply to the full booking or to specific participants, preview the financial effect before saving, and manage applied discounts from one side panel.

## Updates in This Release

* **Reusable Discount Rules:** save named discount setups and apply them again on future bookings.
* **Two application modes:** choose between **Booking total** and **Participant allocation**.
* **Fixed and percentage discounts:** use the discount type that matches the situation.
* **Better operator control:** preview totals, warnings, and customer-facing results before confirming.
* **Legacy separation:** older card-based discounts stay separate from the new rule system.
* **Improved reliability:** applied discount values, stacked discounts, and participant allocations are handled more consistently.
* **Surcharge handling in Diversdesk:** manage surcharges directly in Diversdesk without needing to go to the payment gateway provider.
* **Improved webshop registration for in-house devices:** smoother registration flows for guests signing up on your iPad or other in-house device.
* **Blocking calendar days:** block unavailable days more easily in the planner.
* **Split bill functionality:** divide booking charges more clearly when billing needs to be separated.
* **Easier activity rescheduling:** participants are now selected by default, and you can use **Schedule again** to move faster.
* **Day multiplier selection for tiered activities:** choose the correct day multiplier when adding dives to tiered activities.

---

## Other Improvements Included in This Release

Alongside the discount calculator rollout, this release also improves several day-to-day booking and planning workflows.

### Surcharge handling directly in Diversdesk

Surcharge handling is now improved directly inside Diversdesk. This removes the need to switch to your payment gateway provider just to manage surcharge behaviour.

### Better in-house webshop registration

Webshop registration has been improved for in-house device use, such as guest registration on an iPad at your dive centre. This makes on-site registration smoother and more practical for front-desk use.

### Blocking calendar days

You can now block calendar days more easily, making it clearer when dates should stay unavailable for planning or booking.

### Split bill functionality

Split bill functionality is now included, making it easier to separate charges when one booking needs to be billed across multiple people or payment arrangements.

### Easier rescheduling of activities

Rescheduling is now quicker. Participants are selected by default, and you can use **Schedule again** to repeat the scheduling flow with less manual clicking.

### Day multiplier selection for tiered activities

When adding dives for tiered activities, you can now choose the day multiplier directly. This gives operators more control when building out tiered activity schedules.

### 5 Jun 2026 - Discount calculator enters the app

The first discount calculator rollout entered the app on 5 June 2026.

That early rollout marked the start of the new discount workflow and set the stage for the rule-based side panel that followed later in June.

---

### 16 Jun 2026 - Rule-based calculator panel released

On 16 June, the current side-panel based Discount Calculator flow was added inside the booking process.

From the start, it included:

* reusable Discount Rules
* percentage and fixed amount discounts
* booking-wide and participant-based application
* line selection for activities, add-ons, accommodation, and retail
* preview totals before applying
* customer preview and discount removal tools

This was the point where discount handling moved away from a single simple booking discount field toward a dedicated calculator workflow.

---

### 16 Jun 2026 - Legacy conflict protection

Later the same day, the rollout was tightened to protect bookings that still relied on the older discount method.

The old discount percentage field was deprecated, and the calculator started warning operators when a legacy card discount was already present. If a team intentionally wanted to replace that legacy setup, the action had to be confirmed explicitly.

This reduced the risk of accidentally overwriting older booking discounts.

---

### 17 to 18 Jun 2026 - Legacy discounts separated from new rules

The next updates made the distinction between old and new discount handling much clearer.

Legacy discounts stopped being converted automatically into new Discount Rules. Instead:

* legacy discounts stay visible as legacy entries
* they are edited from the original activity card
* the new calculator focuses only on the reusable rule-based workflow

For operators, this made older bookings safer and made it easier to understand which discounts belonged to the new system.

---

### 19 Jun 2026 - Major calculation improvements

19 June brought the largest set of follow-up improvements.

These updates focused on making discount results more accurate and more dependable when bookings become more complex.

Key improvements included:

* participant discounts now reconcile more reliably
* fixed and scoped discounts are capped more safely against the available balance
* discount calculations stay in sync better when sale lines change
* applied amounts are handled more consistently between the UI and the saved booking data
* participant discounts are split by tax bracket so invoice totals and tax calculations remain correct

This was the main step that turned the first release into a more production-ready tool for real bookings.

---

### 20 Jun 2026 - Safer editing and clearer UI feedback

The next set of updates focused on day-to-day usability.

Operators now get clearer UI hints when working with applied or detached discount states, zero-value discounts are blocked, and legacy stack handling was simplified.

These changes made editing more predictable and reduced the chance of saving incomplete or invalid discounts.

---

### 21 Jun 2026 - Managed discount filtering cleanup

An additional cleanup pass improved how managed discount sales are identified in the background.

This was mainly a reliability improvement, helping the calculator stay focused on the correct discount-related booking data.

---

### 22 Jun 2026 - Participant discounts stay linked after rename

The latest update in this rollout fixed an important follow-up issue: participant discounts now remain linked correctly even after a participant is renamed.

That keeps existing participant-based discounts stable and avoids confusion when guest details are updated after a discount has already been applied.

---

## What This Means for Operators

The Discount Calculator is now the main workflow for new discounts in a booking.

In practice, this means you can:

* save reusable discount rules instead of rebuilding the same discounts repeatedly
* choose whether a discount belongs to the full booking or to specific people
* review warnings before applying stacked or risky discounts
* keep older legacy discounts separate, so historic bookings remain easier to manage
