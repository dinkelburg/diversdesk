Planned Release: July 2, 2026

## Discount Calculator And Split Billing Are Prepared But Not Live Yet

We prepared the release notes for these features already, but they are **not included in today's live release yet**.

We want to smooth out a few final details before enabling them for everyone.

## Planned Features In The Next Update

* **Discount Calculator:** a rule-based discount workflow to replace the old one-field booking discount flow.
* **Split billing:** the ability to separate booking charges more clearly when billing needs to be divided.

---

## What The Discount Calculator Will Include

The new **Discount Calculator** replaces the old one-field discount workflow with a more flexible rule-based system.

When released, operators will be able to:

* save reusable discount rules instead of rebuilding the same discounts repeatedly
* choose whether a discount applies to the **booking total** or to specific **participants**
* use **fixed amounts** or **percentages**
* preview totals, warnings, and customer-facing results before confirming
* keep older legacy discounts separate from the new rule-based workflow

## What Is Still Being Smoothed Out

The remaining release work focuses on final reliability and usability checks, especially around:

* calculation consistency on more complex bookings
* discount allocation behaviour across participants and lines
* the split billing experience in real operator workflows
* making sure the overall side-panel flow feels ready for broad rollout
