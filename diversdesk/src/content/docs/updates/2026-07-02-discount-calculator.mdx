<p class="update-release-date">Scheduled Release: July 3, 2026 (might change)</p>

The next update will include two new features that will improve how discounts and billing are handled in Diversdesk.

* **Discount Calculator:** a rule-based discount workflow to replace the old one-field booking discount flow.
* **Split billing:** the ability to separate booking charges more clearly when billing needs to be divided.

---

## What The Discount Calculator Will Include

The new **Discount Calculator** replaces the old percentage discount workflow with a more flexible rule-based system.

When released, you will be able to:

* Create reusable discount rules instead of rebuilding the same discounts repeatedly
* Choose whether a discount applies to the **booking total** or to specific **participants**
* Use **fixed amounts** or **percentages**
* Preview totals, warnings, and customer-facing results before confirming

## What Split Billing Will Include
The new **Split Billing** workflow will allow you to separate charges more clearly when a booking needs to be divided between multiple parties.

When released, you will be able to:
* Split charges between participants, lines, or other booking elements
* Preview how the split will appear to customers and in your internal totals

## Stay Tuned
This update is currently in the final stages of testing and validation. We will share more details and a full release note once it is live.
