<p class="update-release-date">Release Date: September 1, 2026</p>

This release introduces a connected **liveaboard workflow** in Diversdesk, bringing trip setup, cabin availability, direct and partner sales, guest registration, payments, and operational follow-up into one system.

---

## Updates in This Release

* **Liveaboard itineraries and departures:** configure trip dates, routes, ports, durations, included dives, and departure-specific information.
* **Cabin inventory and pricing:** manage cabin types, individual cabins, availability, occupancy rules, private-cabin options, trip pricing, supplements, fees, and promotions.
* **Liveaboard Reservation Manager:** review availability, create and update reservations, assign cabins and guests, and keep departure bookings together in one operational view.
* **Direct liveaboard sales:** publish departures with current cabin availability and let guests complete booking and registration through the connected customer flow.
* **Partner and agent sales:** manage partner accounts, commercial terms, commissions, pricing adjustments, availability access, booking options, and confirmed reservations.
* **Live availability portal:** approved partners can review available departures and cabins while other bookings remain anonymous.
* **Deposit and balance workflows:** configure payment timing, request deposits or balances, offer supported payment methods, and allow full payment where enabled.
* **Manual payment proof:** guests can upload payment confirmation for review, while operators can approve or reject the submitted proof.
* **Automated liveaboard emails:** send booking, payment, reminder, confirmation, rejection, cancellation, and overdue updates to the configured customer, operator, or partner recipients.

---

### Plan itineraries, departures, cabins, and prices together

Liveaboard operators can now build their sellable schedule around **itineraries and fixed departures**.

Each departure can carry its own dates and operational details, while cabin types and individual cabins define the inventory available for sale. Pricing can be adjusted per departure and cabin type, including occupancy rules, single supplements, mandatory fees, private-cabin pricing, and promotions.

This keeps the product guests and partners see connected to the cabin inventory the operations team manages.

### Manage departure reservations in one place

The new **Liveaboard Reservation Manager** gives teams a departure-focused view of cabins, availability, and bookings.

Staff can create reservations, assign cabins, add or update guests, record the booking source, connect a partner when applicable, and review the bookings attached to a departure. Availability updates from the same reservation data, reducing the need to reconcile separate sales and operations lists.

### Sell through direct and partner channels

Published liveaboard departures can be offered through the connected customer booking flow with current cabin choices and pricing.

For partner sales, operators can create partner accounts and define the commercial relationship for the relevant liveaboard locations. Terms can include commissions, price adjustments, availability access, payment responsibility, and settlement details.

Approved partner users can access a dedicated availability portal, place time-limited booking options, and submit reservations against the inventory made available to them. Diversdesk also supports a liveaboard availability feed for ScubaDates.

### Follow deposits, balances, and payment proof

Liveaboard bookings now support a payment plan based on the operator's deposit and balance policy.

Depending on the configuration, customers can choose from the available payment methods, pay the full amount when permitted, or upload proof for a manual payment. Submitted proof can be reviewed by the operator before the payment is accepted.

Payment status, deadlines, reminders, and booking communication stay connected to the liveaboard booking. Separate recipient settings let operators control which customer, operator, and partner messages are enabled for each step of the journey.
