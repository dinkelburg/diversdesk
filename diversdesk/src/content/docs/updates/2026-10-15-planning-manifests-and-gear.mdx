<p class="update-release-date">Release Date: October 15, 2026</p>

This release makes it easier to prepare for the day: choose the information your team sees, check tank and meal requirements, and spot scheduling conflicts before saving.

It also introduces a new participant panel and rental gear fitting workflow. **These two features are disabled by default and enabled separately for each establishment.** Their availability is explained below, alongside updates for connected accommodation and liveaboard workflows.

---

## Updates in This Release

* **More flexible day manifests:** find, select and reorder columns, scroll wide tables more easily, and include visible staff rows in exports.
* **Clearer tank and meal planning:** review tank quantities by size and blend, spot mismatches, and include staff dietary preferences in trip meal counts.
* **Better planner controls:** choose your planner columns, show payment status, and check for duplicate or overlapping participant assignments.
* **Less repetitive administration:** update eligible registrations to the latest form in bulk and export the complete filtered customer list.
* **More dependable editing and reporting:** protect unsaved panel changes and keep complimentary items visible in sales reports.

The improvements above apply to the relevant existing workflows. Your permissions, selected columns and establishment settings still determine what you see.

## Everyday Planning and Administration

### Put the information your team needs on the manifest

The day manifest's column editor now lets you search for columns, filter the list, and choose what to show. Arrange columns by dragging them or using the move-up and move-down controls.

Wide manifests stay within the page, with a horizontal scrollbar to reach columns further across the table. Exports can include visible staff rows, including their tanks and dietary information.

The planner has its own column settings, including an optional **payment status** column. Planner settings are shared across the establishment, and changing them requires the appropriate permissions.

### Check tanks and meals before the trip

Tank summaries group quantities by **tank size and gas blend**, making it easier to compare what is needed with what has been assigned. Mismatch warnings help you spot quantities that need attention. Use **Match all** for the eligible guest or staff allocations you want to update; mixed allocations need individual review.

Eligible guide tank defaults account for the trip's dive sites. Dry or rest trips and non-diving guide roles do not receive automatic diving tank quantities. If you explicitly save a staff member with no tanks, that choice stays empty.

When the **diet column** is displayed, trip food summaries bring guest and staff requirements together. They show meal counts, dietary preferences, people bringing their own food and missing answers. Guests are counted once per trip, and people bringing their own food are excluded from the meal count.

### Avoid conflicting participant schedules

Trip end times now help Diversdesk check for overlapping participant assignments. Confirmed overlaps are blocked; when missing end times leave a possible conflict, you are asked to confirm before continuing. The scheduling dialog also makes it clearer whether you are moving someone or adding another trip.

Trip and staff filters, calendar opening and staff counts have been refined. Removing a trip from a participant's schedule also removes it from the pending selection.

When the final guest assigned to an instructor or divemaster is removed from a trip, the unused guide assignment is cleared. This keeps staffing, food and tank totals in line with the remaining guests. Captain and crew assignments are preserved.

### Update forms and export complete customer lists

After changing a registration form, you can preview and update eligible upcoming or ongoing bookings and guest registrations that still use an older version of that form. This reduces the need to update each one separately. Cancelled bookings and bookings with an open checkout payment are excluded.

Customer CSV exports now include **all customers matching your filters**, rather than only the page currently displayed. Exports include certification, activity names and activity counts, with improved support for accented characters in Excel. Cancelled bookings are excluded from activity filtering and totals.

### Keep edits safe and complimentary items visible

The affected side panels now ask for confirmation before discarding unsaved edits and prevent closing while a save is in progress. Closing the top panel also leaves the panels underneath in place.

Sales reports now show zero-priced items when a sale or participation quantity has been recorded, so complimentary items remain part of your operational reporting.

## New Tools That Require Activation

**The new participant panel and New gear fitting are separate settings, both disabled by default.** Enabling one does not enable the other. Contact the Diversdesk team to discuss availability for your establishment.

### Participant details and actions in one panel

Where the **new participant panel** is enabled, staff can review contact details, age, diving qualifications, registration progress, activities and documents in one place.

Open or complete a registration, copy its link, view or upload waivers, and manage document approvals without navigating between several screens. Editing actions continue to follow staff permissions.

The panel also includes tank information. Its new rental fitting and assignment tools appear when **New gear fitting** is enabled as well.

### Prepare and assign rental gear by category

Where **New gear fitting** is enabled, the **Daily Gear**, **Gear Sizes** and **Gear Assignment** views help the team prepare equipment. Sizes, assigned items and category notes can each have their own manifest column.

Work through equipment one category at a time, checking available items and fitted sizes. **Save & Next** saves the current category before continuing. You can record when guests use their own equipment, and warnings highlight mixed sizes or items that do not match the fitted size.

Weight fitting includes quick choices from **1–10 kg** and an **Other** option. Assigned weights show their combined total and highlight differences from the recorded requirement.

Internal notes can be saved per gear category without changing the customer's answers. Customer notes and staff notes are shown with their source clearly identified. Staff rental equipment can also be assigned by day from the same inventory, with availability checks to prevent double assignments.

A separate review step shows assigned equipment and warnings before you mark a guest's gear check complete. Opening the review does not complete the check, and a failed save leaves it open so you can retry.

### Guided setup for eligible establishments

Where onboarding is enabled, eligible users can work through business setup, offerings, diving operations and staff details in a guided flow. Optional example customers, bookings and trips provide a way to explore everyday operations. This setup flow is available only to establishments where it has been enabled and has not yet been completed.

## Updates for Connected Services and Liveaboard Operators

These changes apply when you use the relevant integration or booking workflow.

### Clearer accommodation channel management

For establishments connected to **Channex**, the channel-management screens now separate setup, rooms, rate plans, restrictions, bookings and items needing attention.

Manage multiple rate plans per room type, set fixed or percentage price adjustments, and apply date-specific stop-sell and minimum-stay rules. Attention indicators make synchronization problems easier to find.

Bookings imported from online travel agencies also have improved payment and booking-change handling. Payments collected by the booking channel are recorded automatically and protected from manual changes. Cancellation handling distinguishes confirmed retained charges from amounts that still need review.

### Understand partner responsibilities

Authorized booking partners can open a guide for their operator connection explaining who approves bookings, who collects deposits and balances, and who receives booking and payment notifications.

### Share liveaboard availability with Scubadates

Liveaboard operators can opt in to sharing trip information with **Scubadates**, including availability, cabin prices, itineraries, requirements, destinations and onboard fees.

Trip templates support up to three Scubadates regions and itemized per-person onboard fees, including currency and notes. Sharing must be enabled for the establishment.

### Keep the booking widget in the right language

The liveaboard booking widget follows its configured language or the host website's language and keeps that choice as guests move through the booking flow.

Supported interface translations include browsing, cabin selection and checkout, with localized dates and prices. Translated trip and activity content appears where those translations have been configured; otherwise, the original text remains visible.
