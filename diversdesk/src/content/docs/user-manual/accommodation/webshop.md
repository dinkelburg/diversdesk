Use it to review how accommodation products are exposed to customers and how they connect to your internal accommodation setup.

Typical use cases include:

- checking how room types and accommodation options are presented online
- confirming that internal accommodation setup is ready for customer bookings
- reviewing how availability and pricing logic flow into the webshop experience
