The **Pricing** page is where you manage how your activity pricing is configured.

Depending on your setup, this can include:

- base pricing
- pricing variations and rules
- product-specific pricing structures
- supporting price tools and related settings

If you are looking for the new discount-rule workflow, see the **Discount Calculator** article under **Features & Resources**.
