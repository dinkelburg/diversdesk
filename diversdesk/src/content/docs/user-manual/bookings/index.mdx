## Bookings Overview Page
The Bookings page sits at the center of your daily operations. It’s where you manage current and upcoming bookings, log new ones, and keep track of everything happening across any given day or date-range.

### What this page is used for

Use the overview page to:

 - Review all bookings in one list
 - Create new bookings
 - Search for a booking by booking reference
 - View a day or date range to quickly see how many bookings you have received
 - Filter bookings by date, payment status, or registration status
 - Monitor payment progress and participant registration completion
 - Open a booking and continue working on its details

### Registration and payment status badges

The **Registration** column shows the completion state for everyone in a booking:

 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-registration-none">None</span> Nobody in the booking has registered yet.
 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-registration-pending">Pending</span> At least one person in the booking has completed their registration form.
 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-registration-registered">Registered</span> All registration forms and waivers have been completed.

The **Payment** column shows the booking's payment state:

 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-payment-open">Not paid</span> No payments have been made.
 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-payment-deposit">Deposit</span> A partial payment has been made, but an outstanding balance remains.
 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-payment-paid">Paid</span> The booking has been paid in full.
 - <span class="booking-status-badge booking-status-badge-payment-overpaid">Overpaid</span> The booking has a payment amount above its total, for example after a correction was made to a booking that had already been paid in full.

### How to work from the dashboard

Start by narrowing down the list to the bookings you want to review. This is especially useful when your team is preparing for a specific date range, checking recent self-bookings, or following up on pending actions.

The page functions as a control panel for managing your bookings efficiently:

- <strong>Quick-filter buttons:</strong> Select pre-set days or date ranges instantly at the top of the screen.
- <strong>Search bar:</strong> Find a specific booking immediately when you already know what you're looking for.
- <strong>Custom date fields:</strong> Filter for custom periods or future dates outside the standard quick-filters.
- <strong>Status filters:</strong> Focus strictly on bookings that still require your attention.

### Opening a booking

Once you find the correct entry, click its linked booking ID or booking reference to open the full booking record. From there, you can manage participants, activities, billing, payments, and sharing options in more detail.

### Typical use cases

Teams usually use the dashboard to:

 - Find a booking quickly when a customer contacts the shop
 - Review new or recently changed bookings
 - Prepare upcoming trips by checking who is booked and what still needs to be completed
 - Jump into a booking to make changes, take payment, or send booking details
