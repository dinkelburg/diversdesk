Use **Rental Pricing** to decide what guests pay for rental gear and which activities offer it. Each rental price uses an add-on for its name, price, currency, booking access, and activity connections. Editing those settings from Rental Pricing or from Add-ons updates the same add-on.

Rental pricing covers equipment categories, such as BCD or wetsuit. It does not reserve a particular item from your inventory. Create and maintain physical equipment on the [Rental Items page](/user_manual/rentals/rental-items/).

## Create a Rental Price

1. Open **Rental Pricing** and choose **Add rental price** at the top right.
2. In **Connected add-on**, choose **Create new add-on**, or select an eligible existing add-on to reuse its price and settings.
3. Choose **One equipment category** for an individual rental price, or **Equipment set** for a price covering several categories.
4. Select the **Included rental categories**. Categories that already have an individual price are greyed out for another individual price. Hover over a greyed-out category to see the reason. Those categories can still be included in an equipment set.
5. For a new add-on, enter its name, price, currency, and basis. Choose **Who Can Book This?** to allow customers and managers, or managers only.
6. Expand **Connected activities** and select the activities that should offer this rental.
7. Choose **Save rental price**.

An existing add-on is eligible when it is per person, not selected by default, uses a per-day, per-trip, or one-time fee basis, and is not already connected to rental gear. Reusing it preserves its price, booking access, and existing activity connections; review the preselected activities before saving.

### Create Missing Equipment Without Leaving the Form

Choose the orange **Create new** link beside **Included rental categories**. The shared rental-item panel opens on top of your pricing draft. Save the item to return: its category is refreshed and selected automatically when available for the chosen offering. Your unsaved pricing details are kept.

A new physical item can belong to an existing category. If that category already has an individual price, use the existing price or include the category in an equipment set.

## Price Basis and Booking Access

Rental prices are optional and charged per person. They support:

- **Per day:** The rate is multiplied by the itinerary's full duration in days. For example, a rate of 8 per day costs 40 per guest for a five-day itinerary.
- **Per trip:** The rate applies once per guest for the trip.
- **One time fee:** The rate is a one-off charge per guest.

Rental prices cannot use a per-dive basis, a per-booking price default, or be preselected by default. These restrictions also apply when creating a rental add-on from Add-ons.

**Customers and managers** allows guests to select available rental gear during checkout. **Managers only** keeps selection with staff. Rentals must also be connected to the relevant activity to be offered for that activity. Their currency must match the booking currency to appear in the guest rental choices.

## Connect Activities

Expand **Connected activities** to select individual activities. **Bulk select activities...** adds all activities or an entire category without replacing your other selections. A checkmark marks a fully selected group; a split count, such as 2/3, shows a partially selected group. **Clear** removes the activity selections in the draft.

The number beside **Connected activities** shows how many activities are connected. The info icon explains the section. Save to apply connection changes.

## View and Edit a Rental Price

Select a rental price's name to open its details. When opened from Rental Pricing, **Rental gear** starts expanded and shows the offering and included categories. Its count shows the number of connected categories.

Choose **Edit** to change the shared add-on settings, rental categories, and activity connections. One save updates them together. Changes through **Pricing > Add-ons** affect the same rental price.

Drag rows to reorder prices, then choose **Save order**. Rental prices share their order with Add-ons; ordinary add-ons keep their positions when you reorder rental prices.

## Create Rental Add-ons from Add-ons

Open **Pricing > Add-ons**, choose **Create Add-on**, and enable **Use as a rental add-on** under **Rental gear**. Select an offering and its categories, connect activities, and create the add-on. The add-on and rental connection are saved together.

For an existing ordinary add-on, open its details, expand **Rental gear**, and choose **Connect to rental gear**. The rental-price panel opens with that add-on selected. See the [Add-ons guide](/features-resources/add-ons/) for the shared pricing fields.

## What Guests See

Guests choose rental gear per person during checkout or later through **Extras & rental gear**. Individual categories offer **Rent** and **Bring own** choices. Choosing an equipment set replaces individual rentals covered by that set, so the same category is not charged twice.

Selecting a rental price does not select a size or allocate a physical inventory item. Fitting and allocation remain part of your rental operations.
