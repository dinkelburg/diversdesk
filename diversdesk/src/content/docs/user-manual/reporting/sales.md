The **Sales** page helps you review what has been sold inside Diversdesk and trace sales activity over time.

Use this page when you want to:

- review sales records by date or period
- check which bookings, products, or services generated revenue
- investigate unusual sales activity or confirm that charges were created correctly

In practice, this is the page you use when you need a sales-focused view of business activity rather than a booking-by-booking operational view.
