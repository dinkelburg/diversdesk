Use it to review how retail products are exposed to customers online and how they connect to your stock and sales setup.

Typical use cases include:

- checking whether retail products are ready to be sold online
- reviewing how product setup connects to stock management
- confirming that the customer-facing retail shop reflects the products you want to offer
