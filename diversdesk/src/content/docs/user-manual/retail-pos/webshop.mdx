This page acts as the customer-facing retail catalog and product-detail flow.

## When This Tab Is Visible

The Retail tab is shown publicly when retail mode is set to **Checkout**.

Managers can still access the page internally when retail is enabled, even if the public checkout tab is not currently exposed to customers.

## What Customers See

The Retail Webshop can show:

- A hero banner at the top of the page
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Featured products
- Brand filters
- Product detail pages with variant selection

Only products with active product variants are shown in the retail webshop.

## Product Detail Behavior

On a product detail page, customers can:

- Review product images
- Choose available color and size combinations
- See the product price
- Add the selected item to cart

This makes the public retail flow variant-aware instead of showing only one flat product record.

## Internal Staff/Manager Use

When the Retail Webshop is used internally rather than as a pure customer view, it can also support booking-connected sales flows.

Depending on context, staff can:

- Add a retail item directly to an existing booking/order
- Create a new order from the retail item flow

Managers can also access edit and cleanup-related controls outside customer view.

## Category and Featured Behavior

Category setup affects how customers browse the catalog.

Featured items are shown separately on the main retail shop view, helping you surface priority products before a customer drills into a category.

## Hero Banner Images

The Retail hero banner is managed from **Shop Settings**.

If multiple images are uploaded, they rotate automatically on the public page.

## What This Page Depends On

The Retail Webshop reflects setup from your retail catalog, especially:

- Products
- Variants
- Categories
- Brand values
- Featured flags

Use this page to verify that your public retail offer matches the stock and product presentation you want customers to see.
