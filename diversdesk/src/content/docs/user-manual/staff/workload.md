The **Workload** page helps you review staffing pressure and operational workload across your planning setup.

You can use this page when you want to:

- see how work is distributed across instructors or team members
- spot days or periods with unusually high planning pressure
- support staffing decisions based on scheduled operational load

It is especially useful as a management view alongside the more operational **Planner** and **Day Manifest** pages.
