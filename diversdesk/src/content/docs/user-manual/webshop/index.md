The top-level **Webshop** section is the customer-facing online booking and sales layer of Diversdesk.

Use this section when you want to understand how customers interact with your online offers and where webshop-related configuration connects back into operations.

In practice, the webshop experience is built from several parts of the system, including:

- **Activities > Webshop** for activity-related booking offers
- **Accommodation > Webshop** for room and stay offers
- **Retail POS > Webshop** for retail product sales

This page acts as an orientation point for that broader webshop setup rather than describing only one single operational page.
