When someone books your last ocean-view room on Agoda, you need to take it off sale on Booking.com, Airbnb and your own website. With the new **DiversDesk Accommodation Channel Manager**, availability updates automatically across your connected channels as bookings come in.

You can connect to **78 booking channels**, including Booking.com, Airbnb and Agoda, and manage room availability, rates and reservations from DiversDesk.

## Sell rooms in more places from one calendar

Whether a guest books through your DiversDesk webshop or a connected booking channel, the reservation draws from the same room inventory. Your team can see what’s booked and what’s still available without updating each channel by hand. That saves time and reduces the risk of double bookings.

## Manage rates, restrictions and reservations

- **Room rates.** Set up multiple rate plans for each room type and adjust prices by a fixed amount or a percentage.
- **Booking restrictions.** Set a minimum stay for a busy weekend or close specific dates to new bookings on channels that support those rules.
- **Reservations and payments.** See imported reservations alongside direct bookings, review changes and cancellations, and see payments collected by the booking channel on the reservation.
- **Sync issues.** See which updates need attention so your team knows where to look if something hasn’t synced.

## From room booking to dive day

A guest might find your resort on Booking.com and later get in touch about a couple of dives or an airport transfer. With their stay already in DiversDesk, your team can add those arrangements alongside the room booking and see what the guest has planned before they arrive.
