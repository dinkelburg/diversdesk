Getting started with Diversdesk means setting up an environment that fits the way your dive center works. From activities and pricing to customer records and onboarding journeys, there are details to bring together before your team gets going.

Our **USD 128 transfer and setup package** gives you hands-on help with that initial setup, followed by a dedicated call to walk you through your environment.

## How we prepare your environment

- **Review your requirements.** We look at your activities, retail products, add-ons, pricing and customer data, and confirm the setup scope before starting.
- **Share your information.** We provide a shared drive with example templates to help you prepare your information, plus space to upload your existing data for us to review.
- **Configure your environment.** We set up the agreed items, transfer your existing customer base and provide pre-set onboarding journeys.
- **Walk through it together.** Your onboarding call introduces your custom setup so your team can get started with confidence.

## What about bookings you have already accepted?

Future bookings are not included in the USD 128 package. Transferring customer records and recreating upcoming bookings are separate tasks: each booking may also contain activity dates, participants, selected extras, payment details and special requests.

You can enter these bookings yourself, or ask us to help at **IDR 150,000 per hour**. We can review the information available and agree on an estimated amount of work before starting.

### Can bookings be exported from your current software?

The first step is to check whether your current system can export upcoming bookings, for example as a spreadsheet or CSV file. An export can help us assess the work involved, although it does not necessarily mean the bookings can be imported automatically.

If a suitable export is unavailable, manual entry may be needed. Having complete booking details ready helps make this process more efficient.

## Preparing for your setup

- Use the example templates in your shared drive to prepare your activity list, retail products, add-ons and pricing.
- Upload your existing customer data to the shared drive and tell us which system or format it comes from.
- If you are switching software, choose a switch-over date and prepare a list of bookings that will take place afterwards. Once entered, check those bookings against your original records, including dates, participants, deposits and outstanding balances.
